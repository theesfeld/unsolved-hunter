# Skeptic B — Hunt 49 (Gisclon 2026c, zenodo 20835400)
VERDICT: ACCEPT (with one correction, and the scope stated narrowly below)
Novelty: MINOR. This is the first kill I know of for this specific claim. Lux audit 22850757 does not cover it, and I did not do an exhaustive search. But the target is a self-published Zenodo claim, not an established famous claim, and the flaw is standard stats-hygiene.

Script: skB.py (my own). It uses the §4.1 table directly: A=[16,0,11,15], B=[3,5,4,10] over CS/CH/FS/FH.

1. Reproduced the paper's numbers: T1 = 1.98e-5, T2 = 0.003454, T3 = 0.01329, T4 χ² = 13.20 → p = 0.00422, combined χ² = 52.57, df = 8, p = 1.31e-8. The arithmetic matches the paper.
2. Pillar 1 HOLDS. T1 takes p0 = 5/22 from the comparison group as if it were a known constant. That ignores sampling error in p0, so T1 is a degenerate version of T2's data and T2/T1 = 174×. Even using the pooled p0 = 5/64 gives 0.033 (still not a valid test).
3. Pillar 2 HOLDS. All four tests use the same 64 folio labels. T2 is the CH column of T4's table and T3 merges T4's columns into a 2×2. Fisher's method needs independent p-values, so df = 8 is invalid. Dropping only T1 still gives 2.6e-5, which is invalid for the same reason.
4. Pillar 3 HOLDS, with a CORRECTION. Exact enumeration (multivariate hypergeometric, conditioned on margins) of the χ² statistic gives p = 0.00330. The hunter's Monte Carlo figure was 0.0031. Both are above α = 0.003. Bonferroni ×3 on the best of T2–T4 gives 0.0104. T2 alone (0.00345) also fails. The margin over α is thin (0.0033 vs 0.003), so the kill rests on the paper's own threshold, not on there being no signal.
5. Confound HOLDS. The qok*/fachys families are Currier A/B by the paper's own definition (§3.3). Any A/B difference in which plants appear, or in how they were identified, produces the effect. Plant identifications and their galenic classes are assigned by the author, and nothing shows they were made blind to classifier. That is a further source of bias that no p-value can fix.
6. PRE_REG timing: it was written after the first recompute. I don't think it matters. The tests the kill uses (exact omnibus, Bonferroni) are the standard ones and were not tuned. Report it openly anyway.
7. Scope: the kill covers the CERTAIN combined claim, the "CERTAIN" label on T1, and the decipherment's claim to statistical demonstration. It does NOT remove the A/B humoral association. A valid p of about 0.003 is roughly "STRONG" on the paper's own scale. That residual is confounded with Currier A/B and with unblinded plant IDs, so it gives no evidence for the semantic reading ("qok = herba sicca").

Judgment: The paper's CERTAIN status comes from two errors: a circular T1 and a Fisher combination of four dependent views of one 2×4 table. A correct single omnibus test on the same table gives exact p = 0.0033, just above the paper's own CERTAIN cutoff (0.003). So "first statistically demonstrated (CERTAIN) decipherment" fails. What remains is a moderate A-vs-B association in author-assigned plant humors, which cannot support a decipherment. ACCEPT. Novelty is MINOR. Use 0.0033 (exact) in place of 0.0031, and do not claim there is no association at all.
